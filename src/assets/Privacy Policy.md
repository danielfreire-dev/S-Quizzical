### Introduction

Welcome to Quizzical! At Quizzical, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services.

### Information We Collect

#### Quiz Information

Quizzical collects and stores quiz information locally on your device. This includes the questions, answers, settings, and results of the quizzes you take. This information is stored locally to ensure a seamless user experience and to allow you to resume your quizzes at a later time.

#### Usage Data

We use Google Analytics to collect and analyze usage data to better understand how users interact with our website and services. This data includes information about your device, browser, and actions taken on our website. The usage data is collected anonymously and is not linked to any personally identifiable information.

### How We Use Your Information

#### Local Storage

The quiz information stored locally on your device is used solely to provide you with a personalized and seamless quiz experience. This information is not shared with third parties and is only accessible by you.

#### Google Analytics

The usage data collected through Google Analytics is used to:

- Improve the user experience by understanding how users interact with our website and services.
- Identify and fix any issues or bugs in our application.
- Enhance the overall performance and functionality of our website and services.

### Sharing of Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following circumstances:

- **Service Providers**: We may share your information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf, such as Google Analytics for usage data analysis.
- **Legal Requirements**: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or if we believe that such action is necessary to comply with a legal obligation or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Quizzical, our users, or the public.

### Data Retention

The quiz information stored locally on your device is retained for as long as you have the application installed. You can delete this information at any time by clearing your local storage or uninstalling the application.

The usage data collected through Google Analytics is retained for a period of 26 months. After this period, the data is automatically deleted.

- **Portability**: You have the right to request a copy of your personal information in a machine-readable format.
- **Objection**: You have the right to object to the processing of your personal information for marketing purposes.
